\paragraph{Post-hoc stronger similarity baselines and code tests.}
In response to earlier review, we evaluated four stronger baselines post hoc (unregistered, exploratory): (1) \emph{E5-base-v2 retrieval cosine} between candidate trace and gold reference on the $60$-task anchor set ($r = +0.02 \; [-0.15, +0.22]$); (2) \emph{DeBERTa-v3 NLI entailment probability} that the gold reference follows from the candidate on the same $60$ tasks ($r = +0.05 \; [-0.18, +0.25]$); (3) \emph{Code AST sequence similarity} against canonical solutions on $492$ executed \textsc{HumanEval}/\textsc{MBPP+} outputs ($r = +0.143 \; [+0.070, +0.210]$ versus $+0.134 \; [+0.055, +0.208]$ for unigram-F1 and $+0.166 \; [+0.098, +0.230]$ for TF-IDF, all $4{,}000$ bootstrap resamples); and (4) \emph{Symbolic final-answer equivalence} via \texttt{sympy} with string fallback ($r = +0.59 \; [+0.41, +0.76]$), which reads only extracted answers and falls below the $+0.92$ extracted-answer check solely on parse failures.
Unigram-F1's $-0.49$ hard-benchmark inversion in Table~\ref{tab:citwin} is a length confound that reduces to $-0.07$ when output length is controlled.

\paragraph{Completer sweep (\texttt{P-C1}), cross-family replication, and TOST bounds.}
Table~\ref{tab:completer} details the completer-strength sweep across Qwen2.5 $1.5$B--$32$B ($n{=}42$ tasks covered by per-trace completion pairs, $n{=}32$ within-correct) and the \texttt{phi-4} replication.
Although completer base solve rates climb from $0.49$ ($1.5$B) to $0.83$ ($32$B), curve-mean soundness correlation remains flat ($+0.78$ to $+0.88$) and within-correct correlation stays null ($+0.02$ to $+0.23$; a $7$B value of $+0.23$ at $n{=}32$ carries Fisher half-width $\pm 0.35$).
The \texttt{phi-4} completer excludes the $18$ \texttt{phi-4}-generated anchor traces deterministically, leaving zero same-family traces ($r = +0.82 \; [+0.68, +0.91]$ full-set interval, $[+0.68, +0.92]$ matched-set run; Qwen2.5-14B on the same $42$ Qwen-generated traces reads $+0.89 \; [+0.76, +0.97]$; mean process value on excluded traces is $0.776$ vs.\ $0.725$ on matched traces, ruling out selective omission of hard items).
Controlling for $k{=}0$ problem difficulty leaves a partial correlation of $+0.69$ for curve mean, yet controlling for final-answer correctness drops the curve mean to $-0.10 \; [-0.30, +0.22]$ while the extracted-answer check retains $+0.77 \; [+0.55, +0.95]$ ($n{=}60$).
Two one-sided equivalence tests (TOST, $\alpha{=}0.05$, $90\%$ Fisher-$z$ bounds at $n{=}48$) give $[-0.03, +0.43]$ for \tfa{} (largest unexcluded $|r| = 0.43$), $[-0.05, +0.41]$ for $k{=}0.75$ ($0.41$), $[-0.17, +0.31]$ for curve mean ($0.31$), and $[-0.37, +0.10]$ for the referenced LLM judge ($0.37$).
