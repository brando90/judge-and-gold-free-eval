\subsection{Grading rubric and blind protocol}
\label{app:rubric}
Soundness anchors (0--10 scale, verbatim): ``2 = major logical error or unjustified leap; 5 = partly justified, real gaps; 8 = fully valid, at most minor slips.''
Style anchors (0--10 scale, verbatim): ``2 = confusing or rambling; 5 = readable; 8 = clear and well-structured.''
Annotators also answered two binary questions per solution: \emph{answer-correct} (is the final answer correct?) and \emph{lucky-correct} (is the final answer correct even though the reasoning does not soundly support it?).
Blind protocol: annotators saw only the problem statement and the solution text, with no model names, no metric scores, and no other annotator's grades, and items were presented in randomized order.

\subsection{Prefix-repair completer}
\label{app:completer}
The fixed completer is Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct.
At each cut depth $k \in \{0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75\}$ it generates eight completions per cut point, for thirty-two completions per solution.
A cross-family replication uses phi-4 as the completer under the same protocol; it covers 42 of the 60 anchor traces ($n{=}32$ within-correct), and its numbers ship as \texttt{papers/analysis/pv\_completer\_crossfam.json} and \texttt{pv\_completer\_matched.json}.
The 18 uncovered traces are exactly the 18 anchor traces generated by phi-4 itself (\texttt{missing\_by\_trace\_model}: phi-4, 18), so the replication contains zero Phi-family traces by construction and the missingness is deterministic, not selective.
On the matched 42 traces the Qwen completer's curve mean is $+0.89$ $[+0.76, +0.97]$ against phi-4's $+0.82$ $[+0.68, +0.92]$, overlapping intervals.
The Qwen matched-subset value exceeds its full-set $+0.79$ because the excluded phi-4-generated traces are harder to complete.

\subsection{Reliability ceiling}
\label{app:ceiling}
Cross-rater agreement is measured on the shared block of $n{=}20$ solutions graded by both annotators: soundness Pearson $0.80$, Spearman $0.70$, and style Pearson $0.52$ (\texttt{papers/analysis/dependability.json}).
The second rater graded overall quality under an older schema rather than soundness under this paper's rubric, and clarity rather than style, so these figures approximate the ceiling rather than replicate the rubric.
The agreement is also measured on a different sample from the 60-item random label set.
For both reasons the main text treats comparisons to this ceiling as indicative of magnitude rather than exact.
