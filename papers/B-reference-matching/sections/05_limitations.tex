\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

The main limits concern the human anchor, two since-completed registers, the decomposition's point estimates, the completer's family, the unresolved beyond-correctness margin, and the scope of the ranking evidence.

The human anchor is the 60-item anchor set, graded by one annotator who is also an author, with its reliability measured rather than assumed.
A hypothesis-blind annotator external to the author list regraded 30 of the 60 items under the same rubric, on a blinded instrument with model names and machine scores stripped, agreeing at $0.83$ on soundness; this matches the earlier cross-construct estimate from a shared block, which retroactively supports the older anchor (both reliability cells and implied ceilings in Appendix~\ref{app:ceiling}, Table~\ref{tab:robust}).
The residual caveats: the same-rubric replication covers half the anchor items, its intervals are wide at that sample size, so every ceiling comparison in this paper remains indicative only, and its soundness Spearman is compressed by ties rather than by disagreement on levels.
The free-baseline comparison is computed on the same 60 anchor items, so its partial-correlation nulls inherit that set's width, and the completion curve's residual use case, settings where a final answer cannot be extracted or matched, is untested here.

The registered length-matched uninformative-reference control for the answer-line intervention (P-L1; Table~\ref{tab:ledger}) has since run and landed as predicted: the ranking stays collapsed under a length-matched content-free reference.
The exclusion of the variance-compression reading is certified at the item level: the paired worked-minus-padded ranking delta on the twelve-model per-item subset is $+0.41$ $[+0.35, +0.46]$ over 500 items, excluding zero decisively, while the seventeen-model model-level contrast ($-0.11$ padded versus $+0.35$ worked) is CI-wide and reported as a point contrast only (Section~\ref{sec:mechanism}, Table~\ref{tab:robust}).
The mechanistic uses of the answer-line intervention therefore stand with that alternative excluded, though the control tests one boilerplate template at one length, and other uninformative-reference constructions remain untested.
The variance-decomposition shares ($83$--$92\%$) are point estimates from one fixed-effects procedure; interval estimates and robustness checks under alternative decompositions remain open, and the mechanism claim rests on the decomposition and its cross-family replication, with the intervention and its run control as corroboration.
The other register from the earlier draft, the completer-strength sweep (P-C1), also ran and falsified its middle-band prediction: the curve's validity is flat from a 1.5B to a 32B completer, and the within-correct curve mean stays null at every size (Section~\ref{sec:verify}).

The original completer shares a model family with a subset of the graded traces.
A cross-family replication with a phi-4 completer reproduces the curve's magnitude with overlapping intervals, contains zero same-family traces by construction (its missingness is deterministic, not selective), and its full composition accounting, including the matched-subset comparison and the check that the excluded traces are, if anything, easier to complete, is in Appendix~\ref{app:completer}.
The signal remains substantially correctness-mediated under both completers: the within-correct curve means are null for each (Table~\ref{tab:citwin}, Appendix~\ref{app:completer}).

The beyond-correctness margin is unresolved on this anchor set.
Every cheap signal's within-correct value, matching or verification, sits inside wide overlapping intervals with no multiplicity correction across the battery (Table~\ref{tab:citwin}), so no cheap signal here demonstrably grades beyond correctness; the referenced judge reads $-0.14$ within-correct on the random set, inside a wide Fisher interval of $[-0.41, +0.15]$ that straddles zero, consistent with range restriction without any significance claim, and $+0.81$ only on a divergence-selected stratum of the companion audit~\cite{companionA}, where selection inflates within-correct variance by construction.

All anchor-set numbers come from a single canonical analysis script; earlier drafts and decks quoted values from heterogeneous scripts and estimators, and where they differ the canonical values supersede them.
Human-grade validity claims cover one math dataset plus executable code; a second labeled dataset is future work.
Pooled seventeen-model rankings have wide intervals, so we rest the ranking claims on the within-family ladders, which are individually consistent; those ladders have three to five models each, so their Spearman correlations are coarsely quantized, and we read signs and ladder counts rather than magnitudes.
The \textsc{SWE-bench Verified} within-family result rests on a four-model ladder and is a preliminary observation.
