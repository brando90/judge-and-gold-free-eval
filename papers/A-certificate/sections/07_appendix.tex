\section{Full configuration accounting}
\label{app:confusion}
All 25 pressure-tested configurations: 12 math (4 blind, 4 referenced, frontier honest, frontier sabotaged, plus the two sabotaged ladder configurations, audited post-registration) and 13 code (4 blind, 4 referenced, frontier blind, frontier referenced, frontier sabotaged, two sabotaged ladder).
Audited in-domain: the 13 configurations of Table~\ref{tab:h2h} (four blind, four referenced, the honest and sabotaged frontier, the two sabotaged ladder judges, and the trained reward model).
Applying the frozen rule of Appendix~\ref{app:subsample} uniformly yields six passes (the four referenced ladder judges, the honest frontier, and the blind 14B), four fails (blind 3B, soundness partial $0.19$; blind 7B, $0.32$; sabotaged 7B, $0.28$; sabotaged 14B, $0.38$), and three convictions (blind 1.5B, the sabotaged frontier, and the reward model); twelve of the thirteen verdicts match their pressure outcomes per estimand, the blind 14B pass being the single miss.
The sabotaged 7B and 14B audits are confirmatory post-registration additions: the runs were registered pre-run at pipeline commit \texttt{718c399} with the prediction that both would be convicted, that prediction is falsified (neither style partial is significantly positive: $+0.17$ $[-0.06, +0.38]$ and $+0.24$ $[-0.07, +0.47]$), and the resulting fail verdicts, scored after the pressure outcomes were known, are both correct (regrets $0.400$ and $0.324$, in the failed cluster; \texttt{papers/analysis/sabotage\_ladder\_audit.json}).
The registered-era record therefore remains ten of eleven, and the record including the confirmatory additions is twelve of thirteen.
The blind 14B is a false pass: soundness partial $+0.41$ $[+0.12, +0.64]$ against the $0.40$ cut, style partial $-0.21$, yet excess gap $+0.63$ $[+0.42, +0.84]$ and regret $0.324$, both in the failed cluster.
The sabotaged frontier separates on inflation only (its regret is statistically indistinguishable from the honest frontier's, paired difference $+0.014$ $[-0.058, +0.085]$).
Under the post-hoc revised rule of Section~\ref{sec:pressure}, which requires the soundness-partial CI to clear the bar, the blind 14B returns insufficient evidence to certify, and so do the referenced 1.5B (CI $[+0.21, +0.75]$) and referenced 3B ($[+0.36, +0.70]$), whose intervals straddle $0.40$; the revised-rule record on the registered-era eleven is eight predictions (three passes, two fails, three convictions), eight correct, three abstentions.
The two sabotaged-ladder fails are unchanged under the revision (neither soundness partial reaches the $0.40$ cut and neither style partial is significantly positive), so with the confirmatory additions the revised-rule record is ten predictions, ten correct, three abstentions.
That revision was adopted after the false pass was observed and is frozen for the Putnam replication.
Sabotaged-ladder pressure outcomes for reference: sabotaged 7B (math gap $+0.13$ $[-0.13, +0.35]$ ns, regret $0.400$; code $+0.84$, $0.559$) and sabotaged 14B (math gap $+0.61$ $[+0.49, +0.73]$, regret $0.324$; code $+0.96$, $0.426$); the math cells are now audited as above, while the code cells remain unaudited.
Code: the mechanical-target audit rows (within-item correlation with executed tests, bootstrap CIs) are in \texttt{papers/analysis/code\_oracle\_audit.json}, and the code-side pressure summaries (per-configuration excess gaps with CIs, regrets, and tie fractions) are mirrored at \texttt{papers/analysis/bon\_code\_full.json}.
Excluding the two sabotaged code ladder cells, which remain unaudited, leaves eleven eligible code configurations; extrapolated verdicts match the pressure outcome in ten, with the referenced 1.5B the single miss (gap $+1.40$, regret $0.441$), coinciding with its null reference gain on the target axis.
Code verdict predictions were not registered, and the extrapolation scoring is post hoc.

\section{Verdict stability under label subsampling}
\label{app:subsample}
At $n$ labels resampled from the anchor set (500 draws), the fraction of draws reproducing the full-sample verdict under fixed rules (convict if style partial $> 0.30$; pass if soundness partial $> 0.40$ and style partial $< 0.20$): sabotage conviction $0.93/0.98/1.00$ at $n = 20/30/40$; honest frontier pass $0.96/1.00/1.00$; 1.5B-blind conviction $0.65/0.84/0.94$.
The boundary configurations differ from each other: the 1.5B referenced pass reaches $0.70$ at $n{=}40$ and $0.94$ at $n{=}50$, while the 14B blind pass is still at $0.59$ at $n{=}50$ and reproduces fully only at the complete $n{=}60$ set.
Full table: \texttt{papers/analysis/label\_subsampling.md}.
Section~\ref{sec:pressure} shows that the 14B blind pass this rule produces is a false pass under pressure.
