\section{Full configuration accounting}
\label{app:confusion}
This appendix accounts for all 25 pressure-tested configurations and both verdict rules.
The pools: 12 math configurations (4 blind, 4 referenced, honest and sabotaged frontier, two sabotaged ladder) and 13 code (4 blind, 4 referenced, frontier blind, frontier referenced, frontier sabotaged, two sabotaged ladder).
Audited in-domain: the 13 configurations of Table~\ref{tab:h2h}.
Applying the frozen rule uniformly yields six passes (the four referenced ladder judges, the honest frontier, and the blind 14B), four fails (blind 3B, soundness partial $0.19$; blind 7B, $0.32$; sabotaged 7B, $0.28$; sabotaged 14B, $0.38$), and three convictions (blind 1.5B, the sabotaged frontier, and the reward model); twelve of the thirteen verdicts match their pressure outcomes per estimand, the blind 14B pass being the single miss.
The sabotaged 7B and 14B audits are late-scored confirmatory additions (ledger, Table~\ref{tab:ledger}): the registered convict prediction was falsified, and the resulting fail verdicts, scored after the pressure outcomes were known, are both correct (regrets $0.400$ and $0.324$, in the failed cluster).
The registered record therefore remains ten of eleven, and the record including the confirmatory additions is twelve of thirteen.
The blind 14B is a false pass: soundness partial $+0.41$ against the $0.40$ cut, style partial $-0.21$, yet inflation $+0.63$ and regret $0.324$, both in the failed cluster.
The sabotaged frontier separates on inflation only; its regret difference from honest is in Table~\ref{tab:robust}.
Under the post-hoc revised rule of Section~\ref{sec:pressure} (soundness-partial CI must clear the bar), the blind 14B, referenced 1.5B, and referenced 3B all return insufficient evidence (intervals in Table~\ref{tab:robust}, panel G); the revised-rule record on the registered eleven is eight predictions (three passes, two fails, three convictions), eight correct, three abstentions.
The two sabotaged-ladder fails are unchanged under the revision, so with the confirmatory additions the revised-rule record is ten predictions, ten correct, three abstentions.
The revision was adopted after the false pass was observed and is frozen for the Putnam replication.
Sabotaged-ladder pressure outcomes for reference: sabotaged 7B (math inflation $+0.13$, n.s., regret $0.400$; code $+0.84$, $0.559$) and sabotaged 14B (math inflation $+0.61$, regret $0.324$; code $+0.96$, $0.426$); the math cells are audited as above, the code cells remain unaudited.
Code: excluding the two unaudited sabotaged code ladder cells leaves eleven eligible configurations; extrapolated verdicts match the pressure outcome in ten, with the referenced 1.5B the single miss (inflation $+1.40$, regret $0.441$), coinciding with its null reference gain on the target axis; the worst blind code regret is $0.544$.
Code verdict predictions were not registered, and the extrapolation scoring is post hoc.
Pressure-curve values behind Figure~\ref{fig:curves} (raw gap at $n = 4/16/64$): blind 3B code $+0.64 \to +0.94 \to +1.24$; blind 7B code $+0.57 \to +0.95 \to +1.19$; referenced 3B code $+0.33 \to +0.40 \to +0.44$; referenced frontier code $+0.39 \to +0.43 \to +0.48$.

\section{Verdict stability under label subsampling}
\label{app:subsample}
This appendix quantifies how many labels each verdict needs.
At $n$ labels resampled from the anchor set (500 draws), the fraction of draws reproducing the full-sample verdict under fixed rules (convict if style partial $> 0.30$; pass if soundness partial $> 0.40$ and style partial $< 0.20$): sabotage conviction $0.93/0.98/1.00$ at $n = 20/30/40$; honest frontier pass $0.96/1.00/1.00$; 1.5B-blind conviction $0.65/0.84/0.94$.
The boundary configurations differ from each other: the 1.5B referenced pass reaches $0.70$ at $n{=}40$ and $0.94$ at $n{=}50$, while the 14B blind pass is still at $0.59$ at $n{=}50$ and reproduces fully only at the complete $n{=}60$ set.
Section~\ref{sec:pressure} shows that the 14B blind pass this rule produces is a false pass under pressure.

\section{Paired-contrast confidence intervals}
\label{app:cis}
This appendix carries the blind-minus-referenced inflation contrasts and the multi-valid-task inversion.
Blind-minus-referenced inflation contrasts, with per-item bootstrap 95\% CIs.
Math ($n{=}105$): $+0.72$ $[+0.57,+0.86]$, $+0.37$ $[+0.24,+0.51]$, $+0.62$ $[+0.48,+0.76]$, $+0.57$ $[+0.36,+0.78]$ (1.5B/3B/7B/14B).

On the multi-valid tasks the sign inverts: referenced-minus-blind contrasts are positive at all four sizes ($+0.38$ $[-0.09, +0.85]$, $+0.66$, $+0.10$, $+0.49$; individual CIs cross zero at $n{=}51$ items), the raw gaps illustrate the size of the inversion (3B: $+0.55$ blind versus $+1.21$ referenced), and the anchoring-margin mechanism carries CIs excluding zero in all eight referenced cells: among equally valid answers, referenced judges score those matching the shown reference $+2.7$ to $+10.0$ points (of 10) above the rest, with total anchoring at 1.5B--3B.
The static multi-valid reference cost of Section~\ref{sec:audit} ($7$--$8$ points of verdict accuracy) is paired, $p \le .0005$.
