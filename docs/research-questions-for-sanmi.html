Koyejo group · judge-free evaluation

Next research directions: which one should we take?

The TFA study is essentially done. A cheap, reference-anchored signal ranks models well but cannot grade an individual solution, and we can prove why: 89 to 96 percent of the per-item score is reference nuisance, not competence.

Five directions open from there, in no particular order. My own read is at the bottom, kept separate on purpose.

the map

Finish the reference-anchored picture, or prove the no-go

We ruled out single-reference TFA at the item level, but multi-reference, outcome-gated, and per-step variants are untested. We still do not know whether any reference-anchored signal can grade within the set of correct answers.

First testOutcome-gated multi-reference TFA on the datasets where single-reference failed. Either we recover a signal or we close the door formally. Reuses the existing pipeline, so this is days, not weeks.

certification

Check an evaluator before we deploy it, without full ground truth

Today we find out a judge is hackable only after it fails (a stray colon flips GPT-4o up to 35 percent of the time). There is no cheap pre-flight check.

First testRun our nuisance decomposition on an LLM judge (quality vs length, style, position) from a small human-graded anchor set, and see whether the nuisance fraction predicts where the judge gets gamed on JudgeBench. The machinery already exists.

process value

A reference-free signal that grades agents and survives being optimized against

Our rollout process value grades solutions, but it needs a gold trace and costs roughly 100x TFA. Agentic tasks rarely have gold traces, and any reward signal gets gamed once you train against it.

First testRollout value grounded in an environment's own success oracle on SWE-bench-style tasks, then optimize an agent against it and measure how much reward hacking shows up.

robustness curves

Do oversight signals degrade predictably as the solver gets stronger?

Benchmarks score overseers at parity. Nobody measures how reliability bends as the capability gap and the optimization pressure grow, which is the thing that actually decides whether a method scales.

First testFix a task, sweep solver scale, plot judge and TFA reliability against the capability gap, and check whether the style-offset we already measured predicts the slope.

no oracle

Is there any cheap signal where there is no checkable answer at all?

Everything above has a checkable answer. Open-ended work (writing, research proposals, open agentic goals) may have no cheap floor beneath human grading or debate, and we have not tested it.

First testApply the nuisance decomposition to a subjective-grading dataset with human labels, and see whether quality separates from surface at all.

My read (last, on purpose)

If it were my call I would open with certification. It is the cheapest, it reuses the decomposition we already built, and it becomes a concrete result in a couple of weeks, which then feeds the bigger swing in robustness curves. Robustness curves is the higher-ceiling paper but the larger commitment. The map is the safe finisher for the current work. I would treat process value and no oracle as follow-ons once one of the first two lands.
